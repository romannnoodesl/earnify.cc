DOGE vs BTC Payouts: Which Coin Should Publishers Choose?

Browser mining with Earnify lets you get paid in DOGE or BTC. Compare transaction fees, minimum payouts, volatility, and earning speed to pick the best coin for your site's traffic and revenue goals.

The Payout Dilemma: DOGE or BTC?

When you integrate Earnify’s browser-based MinotaurX mining, every share your visitors generate lands in your Zpool account. But here’s the fork in the road: you must choose a payout coin. The pool accepts DOGE (default), BTC, and LTC — all on the same MinotaurX stratum endpoint. The coin you pick shapes your cash-out experience, from how quickly you see earnings to how much of them get eaten by fees.

For publishers, the decision isn't just about coin preference. It’s about matching your traffic patterns, holding strategy, and tolerance for network costs. This guide breaks down the real-world trade-offs between DOGE and BTC payouts, using Earnify’s own 2026 benchmarks and Zpool’s current payout rules, so you can make a data-backed choice.

How Earnify’s Payout Options Work

Earnify’s miner.js is a zero-server WebAssembly miner that runs entirely in visitors’ browsers via Web Workers. All pool communication goes through the mandatory relay at wss://websocket-stratum-server.com, but the actual mining shares are submitted to Zpool’s MinotaurX servers. The payout coin is controlled by the password field in the stratum connection string.

When you call autoMine('yourWallet', 50), Earnify connects to the default pool with c=DOGE. To switch to Bitcoin, you would use the start() function with an explicit stratum object:

earnify.start(
  'minotaurx',
  {
    host: 'minotaurx.na.mine.zpool.ca',
    port: 7019,
    password: 'c=BTC'
  },
  console.log,
  4,
  onWork,
  onHashrate,
  onError
);

No pool migration is needed. The same MinotaurX endpoint handles DOGE, BTC, and LTC payouts — only the password changes. This simplicity makes A/B testing payout coins trivial.

Earnify Insight: Earnify’s dev fee is exactly 1 CPU thread on top of the user threads you allocate — not a percentage split. On a 4-core visitor, you get 3 threads of hashing power and Earnify takes 1, giving you 75% of the total output. This transparent model applies equally regardless of your chosen payout coin.

Transaction Fees & Minimum Payouts: The Hidden Cost

The real difference between DOGE and BTC payouts isn’t the coin price — it’s the friction of moving your earnings off the pool. Zpool imposes minimum payout thresholds and network transaction fees that hit low-volume miners disproportionately hard.

For a site with 1,000 daily visitors, reaching the BTC minimum threshold could take two months. DOGE, on the other hand, hits 50 coins in under a week. And when you finally withdraw, DOGE’s network fee is negligible — often less than a penny — while BTC’s fee can eat 5% of a small payout.

Volatility & Long-Term Holding Strategy

Choosing a payout coin isn’t just about fees — it’s about what you plan to do with the earnings. BTC is digital gold: a deflationary asset with a $500B+ market cap that many publishers hold for months or years. DOGE is an inflationary meme coin with a loyal community and extremely low per-unit value, making it ideal for frequent, small cash-outs.

The Case for BTC

If your site generates consistent, high-volume traffic and you’re willing to let earnings accumulate, BTC’s value stability and long-term appreciation potential are hard to ignore. According to Earnify’s internal data, a news portal with 100,000 daily visitors can mine roughly 0.03 BTC per month. Holding that for a year turns browser mining into a meaningful treasury asset.

The Case for DOGE

Low-traffic sites, tools with short session times, or publishers who want to see daily rewards in their wallet will prefer DOGE. Even 500 daily visitors can generate enough DOGE to trigger a payout every few days. The psychological boost of frequent, tangible earnings can be a strong motivator to keep the miner integrated.

Key Stat: With Earnify’s 1-thread dev fee, a 4-core visitor contributes 3 threads to your hashrate. Across 10,000 daily visitors, that’s the equivalent of 7,500 constant CPU cores mining for you — all without affecting user experience when thread percentages are capped.

FAQs

Can I switch between DOGE and BTC payouts after starting?

Yes. Simply change the password parameter in Earnify’s start() call to c=BTC or c=DOGE. The pool will credit new shares to the new coin, while your existing balance remains in the original coin until you meet its payout threshold.

What is the minimum payout for DOGE on Zpool?

Zpool’s MinotaurX pool requires a minimum of 50 DOGE before a withdrawal is processed. At typical browser mining rates, a site with 10,000 daily visitors reaches this threshold in less than half a day, making DOGE one of the fastest coins to cash out.

Does Earnify charge any extra fees for payouts?

No. Earnify itself has no payout fees, no withdrawal commissions, and no hidden percentages. The only cost is the transparent 1‑thread dev fee that runs alongside user threads. All payout-related fees are determined by the Zpool pool and the respective blockchain network.
