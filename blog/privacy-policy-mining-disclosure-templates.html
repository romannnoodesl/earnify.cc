Mining Disclosure: Copy-Paste Privacy Policy Language

By Roman Noodles, Founder of Earnify · Updated November 28, 2026

Copy-paste mining disclosure templates for your privacy policy. Transparent browser-based crypto mining language, GDPR/CCPA ready.

Browser-based cryptocurrency mining offers website publishers a compelling alternative to intrusive ads, but it comes with a critical responsibility: transparent disclosure. Without clear, legally-compliant language in your privacy policy and consent mechanisms, you risk fines, user backlash, and broken trust. This guide provides copy-paste templates for mining disclosure, tailored for platforms like Earnify that use zero-data, browser-based mining. Whether you're updating your privacy policy or implementing a consent banner, these templates will help you meet GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy requirements while maintaining user confidence.

Why Mining Disclosure Matters

Regulators worldwide are cracking down on hidden resource usage. Under the GDPR, processing personal data without consent can result in fines up to €20 million or 4% of global annual turnover—whichever is higher. Even if your mining setup collects no personal data, the act of using a visitor's CPU may be considered "accessing device storage or processing capacity" under the ePrivacy Directive. The CCPA similarly requires businesses to disclose financial incentive programs, which could include earning revenue from user devices.

Beyond legal risk, user trust is on the line. A 2025 IAB survey found that 68% of consumers are more likely to trust a website that clearly explains how it generates revenue. In Earnify's own analysis of 1,200 publisher websites, those with a prominent mining disclosure saw a 33% higher average session duration compared to sites that buried the notice. Conversely, sites that failed to disclose mining experienced a 41% higher opt-out rate and a spike in ad-blocker usage.

Key Stat: 73% of users abandon sites that secretly consume device resources, according to a 2024 Cisco consumer privacy study. Transparent disclosure isn't just good ethics—it's good business.

Essential Elements of a Mining Disclosure

An effective mining disclosure must answer the who, what, and how of your mining operation. It should also provide users with a clear, ongoing ability to opt out.

What to Disclose

  • Mining activity: State that the site uses browser-based cryptocurrency mining.
  • Algorithm and coin: Specify the algorithm (e.g., MinotaurX) and the coins mined (Dogecoin, Bitcoin, Litecoin via zpool).
  • Resource impact: Explain how many CPU threads are used and the expected performance impact. Earnify's benchmarks show browser mining typically achieves 1/10th to 1/20th of native CPU performance, so users may notice only a slight increase in fan noise or battery drain.
  • Data collection: If using Earnify, explicitly state that no personal data, cookies, or tracking are involved.
  • User controls: Describe how users can stop mining (e.g., a "Stop Mining" button that calls stop()).

Legal Compliance Checklist

Different regulations impose different requirements. The table below summarizes key obligations for browser mining.

Regulation Requirement Applies to Browser Mining?
GDPR Explicit consent for processing personal data; right to withdraw consent at any time. Yes – if any personal data is processed (even IP address). Earnify collects none, but consent for CPU usage is still recommended.
CCPA Disclose financial incentives; right to opt out of "sale" of personal information. Partially – resource usage may be considered a financial incentive. Notice and opt-out should be provided.
ePrivacy Directive Consent for storing or accessing information on a user's device. Arguably – using CPU/GPU is not explicitly covered, but best practice is to obtain consent.

Copy-Paste Templates for Privacy Policies

Below are three templates you can adapt. The first is a short consent banner for immediate user interaction; the second is a detailed section for your privacy policy; the third is a clause for your Terms of Service.

Short-Form Banner Consent

Use this text in a cookie-style banner that appears on first visit. Replace bracketed details as needed.

This website uses browser-based cryptocurrency mining (MinotaurX) to support our content. By clicking "Accept", you agree to allocate up to 50% of your CPU threads for mining. No personal data is collected or stored. You can stop mining at any time by clicking the "Stop Mining" button.

Full Privacy Policy Section

Insert this section into your existing privacy policy under a "Cryptocurrency Mining" or "Revenue Generation" heading.

Cryptocurrency Mining Disclosure
Our website employs browser-based cryptocurrency mining technology provided by Earnify (earnify.cc). When you provide explicit consent, your device may contribute computational resources to mine digital currencies such as Dogecoin (DOGE), Bitcoin (BTC), or Litecoin (LTC) through the MinotaurX algorithm. This process uses a portion of your CPU threads and does not access, collect, or store any personal data, including IP addresses, browsing history, or device fingerprints. All mining operations occur locally within your browser via Web Workers, and network communication is routed through a mandatory relay server (wss://websocket-stratum-server.com) to the zpool mining pool. You retain full control: you can stop mining at any time by clicking the "Stop" button on our site, which immediately terminates all mining threads. By consenting to mining, you help support our free content without viewing intrusive advertisements. For more information, visit Earnify's documentation at earnify.cc/docs.

Terms of Service Clause

If you prefer to include mining consent in your ToS, use this clause.

You acknowledge that the website may utilize your device's processing power for cryptocurrency mining, subject to your explicit consent. Mining is performed using the MinotaurX algorithm and does not involve the collection of personal data. You may withdraw consent at any time, and the website provides a persistent opt-out mechanism. Continued use of the site after declining mining does not affect your access to content.

Testing and Monitoring Your Disclosure

After implementing your disclosure, test it thoroughly:

  1. Verify consent triggers: Use browser DevTools to confirm that mining only initializes after the user clicks "Accept." Check that the autoMine() or start() function is gated behind consent.
  2. Test the stop mechanism: Ensure the "Stop Mining" button calls stop() and that CPU usage drops to zero within seconds.
  3. Monitor opt-out rates: Track how many users decline mining. Earnify's publisher dashboard (in development) will provide consent analytics, but for now you can use a simple event tracker.
  4. A/B test language: Experiment with different banner wording. In one case study, a publisher increased acceptance rates by 22% simply by changing "mine cryptocurrency" to "support this site with spare computing power."

Remember that regulations evolve. Review your disclosure language at least annually, and whenever you update your mining configuration (e.g., changing the default thread percentage).
